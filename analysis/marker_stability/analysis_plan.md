# Does baseline sex-chromosome marker expression predict relative cell recovery at a higher drug concentration?

Internal analysis plan, 21 November 2026 (New York). Written before calculating treated cell counts or treatment contrasts for this analysis. The authoring agent had already read prior Project 59 results, the Tahoe study, sample metadata, column schemas, and the distribution of baseline marker states by organ. This is secondary analysis of a public screen.

## Primary comparison

The marker-positive group contains lines with either Y-gene expression or XIST above the original Project 59 vehicle threshold. The comparison group expresses neither marker above threshold. This grouping concerns measured RNA. No genotype or chromosome count is inferred. The outcome is the change in relative recovery of a line from 0.05 to 5 µM for the same compound, after referencing each well to its plate's vehicle wells. It measures a line's representation among the captured cells. Absolute viability, uptake, growth and killing cannot be separated.

Use every Tahoe-100M plate, its two vehicle wells, and all compounds with both extreme doses. In each well, add 0.5 to the captured count of each of the 50 lines, divide by the total plus 25 and take log base two. Subtract the average log share of the two same-plate DMSO wells for the corresponding line. Keep true zeros in treated wells. Average repeated wells for each compound and dose and line; weight compounds equally. Take the higher minus lower concentration for each line and average across compounds. Within each organ take the mean for marker-positive lines minus the mean for lines with neither marker. Average estimable organ contrasts with equal weights. A negative difference means marker-positive lines lose more relative representation as concentration rises.

The primary panel consists of lines with at least 20 captured cells in each of 28 vehicle wells, in organs retaining at least two lines in each group. All 50 lines remain in the denominator of every well. Test the averaged contrast by permuting entire line labels within eligible organs, preserving group sizes, with 99,999 draws (seed 591121) or full enumeration when smaller. Use a two-sided test against zero, a single primary endpoint, and a model-based 95% Welch–Satterthwaite interval from variation among the lines in each organ and group. The interval assumes independently sampled lines within strata; exchangeability of marker labels is an observational assumption. Results apply to the fixed compound panel and represented organs.

## Checks specified before opening outcomes

- Reconcile plate totals to the sealed full-stream metadata and check SHA-256 for all fourteen count tables. Count retained lines, organs, wells, compounds, zero-recovery entries and excluded conditions.
- Split vehicle wells by numeric sample ID and test the same contrast comparing the larger ID with the smaller. This tests background imbalance tied to control-well ordering.
- Report a biological positive control using adagrasib. Compare annotated KRAS p.G12C lines with other lines within organs containing both groups among vehicle-eligible lines. Estimate the same 5 minus 0.05 µM contrast. Expect greater relative depletion among G12C lines. List exact lines and stratum sizes. This is a direction check, not proof of power for the primary effect.
- Recompute the primary estimate at a minimum of 5 or 50 vehicle cells, with at least one line of each state per organ, with medium/high-confidence vehicle calls only, and after each line and each organ are removed. Retain the original organ when a leave-one-line analysis has at least one line per group. Report state-specific coverage and the effect in each organ.
- List contrasts by concentration and by compound. For repeated treated conditions on multiple plates, compare the per-plate between-state contrast between the lowest-numbered plate and the mean of other plates. Quantify correlation and absolute discrepancy. These repeats do not show biological replication in newly grown cultures. Treat metadata names as recorded and retain ambiguities.
- Report a sensitivity limited to lines and organs with known annotated donor sex, allowing comparisons only where measured state varies within organ and donor sex. Use at least two lines of each state per stratum for this sensitivity; report identifiability.
- Do not use top drugs to make discovery claims or select an alternative primary test after seeing outcomes. Independent cultures with absolute live-cell counts would be required to support a differential viability claim.
