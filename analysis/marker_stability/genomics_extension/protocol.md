# Do marker-matched lines in the same organ respond more similarly to the same drug?

The previous analysis addressed RNA-marker agreement and cell recovery. This exploratory extension was specified before reading autosomal treatment outcomes. The original paper, the earlier results, treatment metadata and available input schemas were known. Only Tahoe's two deliberately repeated plates, 6 and 14, can supply independent plate measurements of an identical drug–dose panel. They use the same well positions; no biological generalization to other batches follows.

1. Take all matched treated drug–dose conditions from the two plates. Aggregate all marker strata for each cell line and well, retain zero counts for undetected genes, and normalize against total captured UMIs across all genes. Select Broad's measured L1000 landmark genes (feature_space = landmark); retain unique matching Ensembl IDs on chromosomes 1 through 22. Use every eligible gene, irrespective of measured response. For each plate–line, pool both DMSO wells for its vehicle denominator, requiring at least 200 cells in each control well. Each eligible treated well must contain at least 200 cells. A response vector is gene-wise log2(1+CPM) after drug minus the same quantity for pooled vehicle on that plate. Keep continuous effects, no threshold or significance filter.
2. Use the fixed 26-line bowel, lung and skin panel from the recovery study (12 baseline Y-positive or XIST-positive, 14 with neither marker), before looking at expression. For two distinct lines from one organ, compare plate 6 of one line to plate 14 of the other using Pearson correlation across the autosomal gene vector. Require observations for both lines on both plates for that condition and average the two crossed orientations. Average across all shared eligible conditions for the pair, requiring at least 20. Count eligibility by organ, line and condition. Restrict the organ estimate to organs with eligible pairs throughout its pair matrix. Within each organ average correlations for the two same-marker groups with equal group weights and subtract the mean correlation of opposite-marker pairs. Average organ contrasts equally. Every biological line can occur in several pairs. Shuffle whole-line marker labels within organ preserving group sizes, using every admissible assignment if feasible, otherwise 99,999 draws (seed 590721). Report two-sided p relative to zero, and effects by organ and leaving one organ out. P-values rely on within-organ exchangeability in these observational lines. Show central 95% of the label-shuffled statistic as a reference distribution, not a confidence interval.
3. For each primary eligible line, compare its plate-6 response to itself on plate 14 for matched drug conditions, and to all other eligible drug conditions on plate 14 giving equal weight to each anchor and then each line. Also contrast matched-plate cross-line responses using the high-ID minus low-ID vehicle wells on each plate. Compute this vehicle contrast with the same organ/marker weights. These controls describe reproducibility and shared baseline noise. Unequal drugs and repeated well positions may contribute to matched concordance.
4. For a biological positive control, triamcinolone appears at 5 µM on both plates. Check five established autosomal glucocorticoid-responsive transcripts FKBP5, TSC22D3, DDIT4, SGK1 and PER1. Report each gene's median drug–vehicle response over matched eligible lines on each plate, and the paired-line median of the within-line five-gene mean. This is a direction check for the assay, with expectation of induction. It does not independently validate the marker contrast. Use all available lines for this direction check and report the count; do not screen other drug endpoints.
5. Check sensitivity to thresholds within the same fixed line panel: use the archived line-level marker thresholds, a 50-cell and 500-cell minimum for treated wells (retain control minimum), and restrict to conditions available for all lines within each organ on both plates. If common conditions are too few, report their counts separately. No substitutions based on outcomes. Remove COLO 205 as an additional, specified sensitivity given the earlier paper. Preserve the effective gene list, compact input matrix, paired outcomes and contributing line/condition counts. Numerical replication must rebuild at least the main statistic independently from saved vectors.
