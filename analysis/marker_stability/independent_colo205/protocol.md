# COLO 205 outside the Tahoe screen

The earlier fellowship already measured basal Y and XIST RNA in Tahoe and compared other reported stocks. COLO 205 was Yp-low and Yq-high in the original RNA panel, with an X-only amelogenin STR result. The two original cutoffs, archived measurements, and this line’s concentration of treated-well disagreements are known. The present observational question is whether independent laboratory RNA and multi-locus DNA assays support a reproducible within-Y difference. Public metadata were searched and read before the new assay values.

Use explicitly named COLO 205 samples (CVCL_0218, CCL-222). Exclude COLO 201 and patient/PDX samples. For new RNA, include the independent IRIC ATCC RNA-seq sample GSM5857280 and other eligible series-defined, non-drug baseline samples if identified as a matched genomic series. Read all eight original Y genes (RPS4Y1, ZFY, DDX3Y, KDM5D, UTY, EIF1AY, USP9Y, NLGN4Y) and XIST, recording missing versus zero. Report measurements in their published units, alongside ACTB, GAPDH, RPLP0 as technical reference genes. Report medians of log2(1+TPM) for the two Yp and six Yq markers only after verifying their arm assignments. Do not import Tahoe’s decision thresholds onto bulk RNA. If a candidate is co-extracted or same source panel, describe the strongest documentary evidence and uncertainty, rather than equating a cell-line name with a shared vial.

For DNA, extract all uniquely mapped chromosome-Y probes from GSE48568, including both COLO 205 array records, and assess the second genomic series GSE55832 for measurable Y coordinates. Use full platform and submitter measurements, including autosomal reference probes and X. Separate non-pseudoautosomal short and long arms after checking each assembly's PAR and centromere coordinates. Summarize sample median log ratio per arm both before and after subtracting the autosomal median. The within-array Yq-minus-Yp difference is the main descriptive contrast. For similarly assayed comparison lines use HCT 116 (XY STR) and HT-29 (X-only STR) from the same panel, including every available replicate and every other array as contextual distribution when suitable. Do not infer absolute chromosome number from relative array signals, particularly female-reference or repeated/homologous probes. Use raw sample-channel intensities and technical flags as a sensitivity if sex of the common reference or background makes ratios unreliable. Compare arm differences in control lines rather than assuming a donor annotation defines their measurement. Probe-level tests are inappropriate for a single cell line. Preserve raw-source URLs, checksums, arrays/coverage counts, and absolute-versus-relative assay units. Report absent/insufficient Y coverage for failed assay candidates and historical cytogenetics only when the published finding explicitly concerns COLO 205.
