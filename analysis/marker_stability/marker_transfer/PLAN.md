# Do vehicle RNA-marker calls transfer to drug-exposed wells?

21 November 2026, New York. This secondary measurement analysis was specified after seeing the relative-recovery estimate and the plate limitations. At specification, only the schema, one metadata record, the original vehicle thresholds and the existing Part A results had been inspected. No treated-marker outcomes had been computed. This is an exploratory analysis working from a publicly available, previously studied atlas.

For every line and well, sum total UMIs, XIST and the original eight Y genes over the full-filtered cells assigned to the line. Use the two published line-by-plate cutoffs learned in vehicle cells; Y is the mean of eight log2(CPM+1) values. Compare the two binary marker calls jointly. The four combinations are expression measurements. They say nothing directly about DNA copy number. Keep the input atlas's QC; introducing the old cell-level filter conditional on these same marker counts would partly select the outcome.

For a well on plate p, compute the line reference by pooling vehicle cells on the other 13 plates. These cells do not contribute counts to the test well, although the original thresholds have seen all vehicle plates. Keep line-wells with at least 200 cells, consistent with the original call minimum. Give every eligible cell line equal weight in the primary summary by averaging its fraction of agreeing drug-exposed wells. Also report the pooled fraction, the 50-/500-cell sensitivities, and the fractions for vehicle wells, each measured marker, individual lines and plates. Report the fractions of all possible line-wells eligible. A pooled vehicle RNA call agreeing with the original line classification is a pipeline check. For missing or undercovered line-wells no expression agreement is inferred.

Compare treated and vehicle agreement among lines represented in both. Present the two rates and their difference (percentage points), same lines weighted equally. A descriptive percentile interval resampling whole lines is conditional on the finite compounds and this atlas. Sample 10,000 times with fixed seed 20261121. A secondary same-plate comparison uses the two pooled vehicle wells as the reference for treated wells if their combined count is at least 200. Compare the same eligible treated wells to both references. As a read-depth check, match the treated and same-plate reference pseudobulks to the smaller library by multivariate hypergeometric thinning (nine marker counts plus all other UMIs, one fixed-seed draw). This checks molecule sampling, not composition, biological replication or batch effects.

Across plates 6 and 14, identify exactly matched compound-concentration and cell-line entries with at least 200 cells in both. Tabulate agreement between their joint calls, whether both plates disagree with the frozen published vehicle pattern in the same new direction, and a breakdown by cell line. A reproducible difference is a lead for a new culture and an orthogonal assay. It cannot distinguish stable genotype changes, transient transcription, ambient RNA, plate effects, or selection of pre-existing cells. No drug-specific hypothesis test, whole-genome conclusion or clinical claim.
