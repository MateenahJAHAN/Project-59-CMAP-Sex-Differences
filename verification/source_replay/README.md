# Reproducing Project 59 from source data

The reference is the [released manuscript and supplement](../../manuscript/). Results below distinguish the archived analysis from the new runs.

| Analysis | Result from the source-data replay |
| --- | --- |
| LINCS2020 and GTEx | All 450,255,544 expression values across 36,523 signatures matched the provider's Level 5 matrix exactly. The GTEx v8 tissue file also matched. [LINCS](lincs_inputs/comparison.json) · [GTEx](lincs_inputs/gtex_comparison.json) |
| Phase 2 | All 50 original compound classifications reproduced. For AS-605240 in liver, restricting the correction to tissues with positive overlap gives an adjusted p of 0.0247. Including all 44 eligible tissues gives 0.0544. [All 2,200 tissue comparisons](phases2_5/comparison/phase2_all_tissue_tests.csv) |
| Phase 3 | All 86 eligible compound–lineage pairs reproduced. Among 50 compounds, fostamatinib moved from other-tissue only to own-tissue enrichment and DL-PDMP gained an other-tissue hit. Both results crossed the FDR threshold when only tied genes were reordered. [3,784-test comparison](phases2_5/RESULTS.md) · [Figure](phases2_5/figures/phase3_within_lineage_counts.png) |
| Phase 4 | Of 53,064 tests, the archive and replay agreed on 9,125 enrichments. Fifty-four archived calls dropped and 35 appeared in the replay. Every compound retained its best category. The executed notebook ranked all mapped genes. [All changed calls](phases2_5/comparison/phase4_changed_threshold_calls.csv) · [Figure](phases2_5/figures/phase4_tested_reference_sets.png) |
| Phase 5 | All 39,996 categories reproduced, including one own-tissue hit and 79 other-tissue hits among 620 completed tests. GSEApy 1.3.1 reproduced every saved score. Version 1.2.1 changed 14 FDR values by at most 0.001. The sign of the pooled enrichment score cannot identify which donor group responded more. [Figure](phases2_5/figures/phase5_testable_tissues.png) · [Results](phases2_5/sensitivity_gseapy_1.3.1/comparison/phase5.json) · [Version comparison](phases2_5/comparison/phase5_software_versions.csv) |
| Bulk RNA | All 1,103 line classifications matched after rebuilding the two source expression tables. Two CCLE Y scores changed by at most 6.94 × 10⁻¹⁸. [Seven output comparisons](bulk_RNA/comparison.json) · [Source files](bulk_RNA/sources.json) |
| Inferred LINCS Y scores | Across 2,800 control wells from 127 lines, every archived line median reproduced at the reported precision. The inferred score distinguished 36 XY from 19 Y-lost lines with AUC 0.534. Its Spearman correlation with bulk CCLE Y expression was 0.213 across 124 lines. [Cohort, estimates and verification](l1000/) |
| Part A vehicle cells | All 130,498 EmeraldBay vehicle and time-zero records and all 23,570 sci-Plex vehicle records reproduced. All 52 EmeraldBay line calls matched. All 1,910 Tahoe source shards yielded 2,205,786 eligible vehicle cells. Every one of the 7,640 per-shard output files matched the archive byte for byte. [Tahoe extraction](part_a/receipts/tahoe_raw_shards.json) · [Cell and atlas comparisons](part_a/) |
| Part B response data | Across 41 lines and 114 tested compounds, the rebuilt inputs reproduced the panel tests for Y (p = 0.7231) and XIST (p = 0.1127). The XIST vehicle control also reproduced (p = 0.0323). All 232 compound and control rows matched, including all 42 reported fields. [Panel tests](part_b/direct_H1.tsv) · [Pooled effects](part_b/direct_H3.tsv) · [Row comparisons](part_b/direct_verification.json) |
| Part A cross-culture agreement | Among 49 lines with data in both Tahoe and bulk RNA-seq, 47 matched on the expressed markers and 46 matched on the state name. A498 expressed XIST alone in both. The Tahoe and bulk rules assign different names to that pattern. The independent EmeraldBay comparison reproduced 40 of 41 matches among confident calls. [Counts and discordant lines](claim_audit/) · [Manuscript, Table 2](../../manuscript/manuscript.pdf) |
| Manuscript figures | Figures 1, 3, S1 and S2 reproduced byte for byte from their respective source inputs. Figure 4 also reproduced all 30,000 permutation draws and its 12 interval estimates. [Figures, plotted data and PDF comparisons](figures/) · [Figure 4 permutations](part_b/figure4_nulls_check.json) |

The Part A Tahoe caller comparison, the extended Part B controls and sensitivity analyses, and the fresh 1,000-replicate power simulation are in progress. The revised [manuscript](../../manuscript/manuscript.pdf) and [supplement](../../manuscript/supplement.pdf) distinguish single-marker counts from joint-marker states. Among measured GDSC2/PRISM lines, 272 of 579 male-labelled lines showed low Y and 125 of 300 female-labelled lines showed low XIST. The earlier numerators, 256 and 122, counted lines with both markers low. Note S10 gives the missing-data counts and explains the exclusion of HCT116 from the separate LINCS comparison.
