## The two compounds that changed Phase 3 category

- **Fostamatinib** gained an own-tissue hit. The blood-derived cell lines enriched the GTEx EBV-transformed lymphocyte set (NES 1.18534, FDR 0.0410). Of 12,276 ranked genes, 12,002 shared their score with another gene. Reordering only ties gave FDR values of 0.0336, 0.0757, 0.0745. The archived file stores two other-tissue scores for this drug. [Full notebook replay and comparison](phase3_literal/archived_and_literal_scores.csv) · [Tie order results](phase3_literal/home_tissue_scores.csv)
- **DL-PDMP** moved from no hit to other-tissue only. Large-intestine cell lines enriched the same EBV-transformed lymphocyte set (NES 1.32482, FDR 0.0355). Reordering only ties gave FDR values of 0.0010, 0.0709, 0.0348. The archive contains the no-hit classification and no individual tissue scores for this drug. [Full notebook replay and comparison](phase3_dl_pdmp/archived_and_literal_scores.csv) · [Tie order results](phase3_dl_pdmp/target_tissue_scores.csv)

The original notebook functions and original cmapPy reader reproduced these two compounds’ fresh results. Each tie-order test preserved the gene scores, seed 42 and 1,000 GSEA permutations. The archive retains 65 individually scored records out of 208 archived hits. Absent scores for unreported tissues cannot be numerically reconstructed from that export. The full replay yielded 209 hits. [Archived scores paired with the replay](comparison/phase3_archived_leading_evidence.csv) · [All replayed hits](comparison/phase3_replayed_significant_tissues.csv)

## The Phase 4 permutation check

For NVP-BEZ235, the placenta comparison used 12 signatures from one female-labelled line. The female-biased GTEx minor salivary gland set had archived NES −1.747 and FDR 0.005. The notebook replay gave NES −1.000 and FDR 0.503. The observed enrichment score stayed at −0.280708 under three other permutation seeds, each of which gave FDR 0.005. Increasing the permutation count from 200 to 1,000 and 2,000 with the original seed gave FDRs of 0.001 and 0.0005. This demonstrates sensitivity for this set. It cannot identify the cause of every changed call. The GTEx direction label describes the reference tissue donors. These pooled CMap profiles do not estimate a treatment difference by donor sex. [Test results](phase4_literal/target_seed_tests.csv) · [All 88 sets](phase4_literal/archived_and_replay.csv)

All 603 pair-level signature counts agreed with their archived counts. The archive and replay each recorded 81 pairs without a hit. Eighty of those pairs were the same. Fostamatinib in placenta gained at least one hit, and thalidomide in pancreatic lines lost its last hit. The pair-level A/B/C counts changed for 56 of 603 pairs. Collapsing to the best category across all tested pairs changed 0 of 50 compounds. [Pair counts](comparison/phase4_pair_counts.csv) · [Compound counts](comparison/phase4_compounds.csv)
